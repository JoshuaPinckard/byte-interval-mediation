## Findings and corrections

1. **Admission is not continuous validation.** The original I2 said all observations were unexpired at commit. `applyPatch` checks the read set before patch derivation and coverage selection; `_publishOperation` rechecks the private scope, not the complete read set's lease expiry. A controlled clock advance after admission still allows commit. The paper now states admission-time validation rather than a publication deadline.
2. **Cross-file freshness needs a coverage assumption.** An unmediated writer can change an observed dependency after validation while the target patch still commits. The code's authority lock does not fence such a writer. The paper now makes the same-authority assumption apply to every observed resource, not just the target file.
3. **Source/test evidence is not final-release acceptance.** The available engine receipts pin `c34cab8c`. They do not attest the final installed 1.0.44 cut. Deployment wording now names the inspected snapshot and coding workflow. Reported host-branch measurements remain separate from this extraction and from deployed-product evidence.
4. **Disjoint writes and recovery need qualifications.** Rebased observations can survive; disjoint write spans alone do not guarantee admission with a broader stale read set. Pending no-ops are also distinguished from aborted before-image recovery. Core retention is distinguished from the separate host branch's pruning.

No engine or application source was changed. The reference authority and its five suites remain untouched.
